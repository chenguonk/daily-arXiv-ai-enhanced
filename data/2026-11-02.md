### [1] [Completeness and dynamical stability in exoplanet occurrence rates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40964)
*Thomas A. Baycroft,Manu Stalport,Romain Eltschinger,Matteo Pinamonti,Jeanne Davoult,Khaled Al Moulla,Yann Alibert*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文用合成系外行星系统测试“平均完备度”与“逐个行星完备度”两种发生率计算方式，并检验加入动力学稳定性约束是否改善结果；结论是平均完备度更可靠，而动力学稳定性约束会引入偏差、不建议使用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 系外行星族群研究常用“发生率”（occurrence rate）量化，其值由探测率乘以完备度修正得到。完备度可基于单颗恒星的探测极限逐个行星计算，也可在参数空间某区域内取平均，但两种做法孰优孰劣尚不明确；同时，把动力学稳定性条件纳入探测极限是否能改进发生率结果也未经验证。

Method: 构建合成（synthetic）系外行星系统样本，对每个样本分别用“平均完备度”和“逐个行星完备度”计算发生率，并与拥有完美信息时的真实值（ground truth）比较，评估各方法接近真值的程度；另在探测极限中加入动力学稳定性约束，考察其对发生率的影响。

Result: 在合成样本中，平均完备度总体更可靠，但当所考察的参数空间区域内潜在族群分布存在陡变时可能出问题；使用动力学稳定性条件会使发生率产生偏差。

Conclusion: 推荐采用平均完备度计算系外行星发生率（需注意参数空间区域内族群分布无陡变的前提），而不建议在探测极限中引入动力学稳定性约束。

Abstract: Exoplanet population studies are often quantified through the "occurrence rate" of certain types of planet. This value can be calculated from a survey of a sample of stars by taking the "detection rate" and correcting with a completeness factor. This completeness is calculated via detection limits on each individual star, and can be calculated either individually for each detected planet or averaged in a certain area of parameter space. We test whether either the average completeness or individual completeness performs better than the other. We also test whether including dynamical stability constraints in the detection limits improves the occurrence rate results. We use synthetic sets of exoplanetary systems and calculate occurrence rates for each and compare to what the value would be with perfect information. We compare how close each method is to the ground truth to assess which is most suitable. Overall, in our synthetic sets, the average completeness is more reliable though it can be a problem if there is a sharp change in the underlying population within the area of parameter-space considered. We find that using dynamical stability conditions causes a bias in the occurrence rate and do not recommend using these.

### [2] [From Anoxic to Oxic Earth: Detectability of Atmospheric Biosignatures across Stellar Hosts with ELT/ANDES](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41621)
*Dwaipayan Dubey,Spandan Dash,Liton Majumdar*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 研究地球大气演化各阶段（太古宙、元古宙、现代）及不同恒星宿主（FGKM）下O₂、CH₄、H₂O、CO₂的探测性，发现M/K矮星周围存在显著的O₂可探测性对比，而类太阳恒星在YJH波段不明显。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有生物标志探测性研究多聚焦现代地球，对早期大气状态（无氧甲烷大气、氧化过渡阶段）及恒星环境的影响相对缺乏探索，需评估大气演化如何影响生物标志的可探测性与有氧/无氧大气的区分。

Method: 构建两套互补光化学网格：围绕太阳的太古宙/元古宙/现代代表情景，以及3.9、2.0、0.8 Ga与现代地球四个地质时期在FGKM宿主下的模型；结合一维光化学建模、高分辨率透射光谱模拟与仪器噪声建模，在ELT/ANDES乐观光子噪声条件下评估分子可探测性（5σ判据）。

Result: 太阳宿主太古宙与元古宙模型需约33与42次凌星使CH₄达5σ，O₂在观测限内低于3σ；现代地球O₂约38次凌星达3σ。FGKM网格中3.9 Ga模型O₂均未达5σ；M3V、K2V、M8V代理下现代O₂分别在9、15、20次凌星达5σ，CH₄在3.9 Ga需11、12、28次，氧化阶段仅需2–4次。

Conclusion: M与K矮星周围存在强烈的O₂可探测性对比，类太阳恒星在YJH波段则无此对比；稳健的生物标志解读需结合互补分子约束（包括信息性非探测）并置于大气演化与恒星环境背景中。

Abstract: Earth's atmosphere evolved from an anoxic, methane-bearing Archean state through Proterozoic oxygenation to the O$_2$-rich modern atmosphere. However, most biosignature detectability studies focus on modern Earth, leaving earlier atmospheric states comparatively unexplored. We investigate how atmospheric evolution and stellar environments influence the detectability of O$_2$, CH$_4$, H$_2$O, and CO$_2$, and their potential to distinguish anoxic from oxygenated atmospheres. We construct two complementary photochemical grids: representative Archean, Proterozoic, and modern-Earth scenarios around the Sun, and four geological epochs (3.9, 2.0, 0.8 Ga, and modern Earth) across FGKM hosts. Combining one-dimensional photochemical modeling, high-resolution transmission spectral simulations, and instrument-noise modeling, we assess molecular detectability with ELT/ANDES under optimistic photon-noise-limited conditions. For solar-host Archean and Proterozoic models, CH$_4$ requires approximately 33 and 42 transits, respectively, to reach the adopted 5$σ$ criterion, whereas O$_2$ remains below 3$σ$ within the observing limits. Modern-Earth O$_2$ reaches 3$σ$ after approximately 38 transits. Across the FGKM grid, O$_2$ does not reach 5$σ$ for the 3.9 Ga models. However, For the M3V, K2V, and M8V proxies, modern-Earth O$_2$ reaches 5$σ$ in 9, 15, and 20 transits, respectively, whereas CH$_4$ requires 11, 12, and 28 transits at 3.9 Ga and only 2--4 transits across the oxygenated epochs. These results reveal a strong O$_2$ detectability contrast around M and K-dwarfs, but not Sun-like hosts in the YJH bands. Robust biosignature interpretation requires complementary molecular constraints, including informative non-detections, within the context of atmospheric evolution and stellar environment.

### [3] [An end-to-end CNN search for ultra-short-period planets in TESS: from the blend ceiling to the independent validation of TOI-6662 b](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42009)
*Juan F. Pagés Navarrete*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 卷积神经网络在传统合成数据集上ROC-AUC达0.97，但换用真实被TESS否决的假阳性后降至0.50（随机水平）。原因是42%假阳性为稀释混合，光变曲线与浅凌星无法区分，理论上限为0.79。作者构建级联管线CASCADE-USP，结合差分像质心分析、高分辨率成像与Gaia天体测量，从其435个未分辨候选者中分离出20个，19个被证伪，第20个为TOI-6662 b——一颗绕M矮星的类地行星。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统系外行星探测基准使用合成凌星作为正样本、食双星作为负样本，但这一设定可能不反映真实难点。作者希望检验该基准的有效性，并应对真实假阳性中存在的物理不可分辨情形。

Method: 先以CNN在合成凌星/食双星数据集上训练并测试；再用TFO真实否决的假阳性替换负样本重测；对145个真实假阳性做像素级分析以量化混合比例与理论上限；构建CASCADE-USP级联管线：CNN＋差分像质心分析＋高分辨率成像场景建模＋Gaia天体测量，对435个未分辨TESS候选者进行优先级排序。

Result: 传统基准AUC=0.97；真实假阳性基准AUC=0.50。42%的假阳性为稀释混合，理论上限AUC=1-f/2=0.79；真实假阳性重训达0.80±0.06，独立验证上限。CASCADE-USP从435个候选中分离出20个，19个被确认是光学上不可见的假阳性，第20个为TOI-6662 b（0.94±0.03 R⊕，周期7.05小时，绕M矮星）。

Conclusion: 单纯依赖单波段光变曲线的分类器无法超越约0.79的AUC上限，因为稀释混合在物理上与浅凌星不可分；需将分类器与像质心、高分辨率成像和天体测量等正交证据串联的多阶段管线才能有效筛选。CASCADE-USP输出的是带证据缺口的优先级排序而非终判，其最后一步外部验证与同时期独立工作一致。

Abstract: We trained a convolutional neural network to detect ultra-short-period planets in TESS photometry, following the procedure now standard in the field: synthetic transits as positives, eclipsing binaries as negatives. Measured that way it reaches an area under the ROC curve of 0.97. Keeping the same model and the same planets, but replacing the negatives with false positives that the TESS Follow-up Observing Program has actually rejected, the area falls to 0.50 -- the value of a random classifier. The conventional benchmark had been measuring a problem that does not exist.
  The reason is physical, and it bounds what is possible. Of 145 such false positives examined at pixel level, 42% are blends: eclipses belonging to a neighbouring star, diluted into the target aperture. Their light curves are indistinguishable from shallow transits, capping the attainable area at 1 - f/2 = 0.79. Retraining on real false positives reaches 0.80 +/- 0.06, confirming the ceiling independently. No classifier reading a single-band light curve can do better.
  A detector alone is the wrong instrument. We built CASCADE-USP, a pipeline chaining the network with difference-image centroid analysis, scene modelling with high-resolution imaging and the Gaia astrometric solution, supplying in sequence what the light curve lacks. Its output is a prioritisation, not a verdict: it records what evidence each candidate still needs. Only the classifier is domain-specific; the other stages apply to any transit candidate. Of 435 unresolved TESS candidates it isolates 20; 19 prove false positives, traced to causes invisible in TESS photometry. The twentieth is TOI-6662 b, an Earth-sized planet (0.94 +/- 0.03 Earth radii) orbiting an M dwarf every 7.05 hours. Our validation, independent of a concurrent one, agrees with it in planetary parameters and classification, closing the CASCADE procedure with an external check.

### [4] [Distinguishing N$_2$-Dominated from O$_2$-Dominated Atmospheres on Terrestrial Exoplanets with Reflected-Light Spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42035)
*Nick Burri,Mario Damiano,Renyu Hu*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 利用ExoReL$^\Re$贝叶斯反演框架证明，纳入O$_2$-O$_2$碰撞诱导吸收（O$_4$波段）可打破大气成分-云-地表-重力简并，正确识别地球类似体的N$_2$主导、富O$_2$大气；要求近红外覆盖至~1.7 μm且0.75 μm处SNR≳20。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 地球大小系外行星反射光谱有望探测宜居性与生物标志，但O$_2$既可由生物也可由非生物过程产生，且反射光反演在成分、云、地表反射率与重力间存在简并，导致氧气解释困难。

Method: 采用ExoReL$^\Re$贝叶斯反演框架，引入(i) O$_2$-O$_2$碰撞诱导吸收（O$_4$波段）与(ii) 波长相关的阶跃式地表反照率参数化；以现代地球类似体为对象，在0.25–1.8 μm光谱上对比含/不含O$_2$-O$_2$ CIA的反演，并针对两种HWO观测构型（R_VIS=140高分辨可见、三宽带分箱）进行信噪比扫描。

Result: 省略O$_2$-O$_2$ CIA时反演可用错误的O$_2$主导大气拟合光谱；纳入后借助O$_4$波段打破简并，无需严格质量先验即可恢复正确的N$_2$主导富O$_2$解。以≥95%后验质量落在N$_2$主导区为稳健判据，两种构型均需0.75 μm处SNR≳20（R_NIR=70时1.27 μm O$_4$带附近约SNR≈14），云量增大会提高阈值；近红外覆盖减至1.4 μm时全SNR范围均无法恢复正确解，反而偏向虚假的O$_2$主导、富CO$_2$大气。

Conclusion: 对地球类行星的稳健氧气解释需近红外覆盖至~1.7 μm且0.75 μm处SNR≳20；O$_4$波段与近红外覆盖是打破简并、区分生物与非生物氧的关键。

Abstract: Reflected-light spectra of Earth-sized exoplanets promise to probe habitability and biosignatures, but interpreting atmospheric oxygen can be challenging because $O_2$ can arise from both biological and abiotic processes. In addition, reflected-light retrievals exhibit degeneracies among atmospheric composition, clouds, surface reflectance, and gravity. We use the Bayesian retrieval framework ExoReL$^\Re$, leveraging recent model developments that incorporate (i) $O_2$-$O_2$ collision-induced absorption (CIA), which produces the $O_4$ bands, and (ii) a wavelength-dependent (step-function) surface albedo parameterization. Focusing on a modern Earth analog, we compare retrievals with and without $O_2$-$O_2$ CIA using spectra within 0.25-1.8 $μ$m, and perform signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) sweeps for two observing configurations motivated by the Habitable Worlds Observatory: a resolved-visible case ($R_{VIS}=140$) and a broadband-visible case (three broad bins). Omitting $O_2$-$O_2$ CIA allows the retrieval to fit the spectrum with an incorrect $O_2$-dominated atmosphere. Including $O_2$-$O_2$ CIA leverages the $O_4$ bands to break this degeneracy and recover the correct $N_2$-dominated, $O_2$-rich solution without requiring a tight mass prior. Adopting a robustness criterion of $\ge95\%$ posterior mass in the $N_2$-dominated region, both configurations require SNR $\gtrsim20$ at 0.75 $μ$m (SNR $\approx14$ near the 1.27 $μ$m $O_4$ band for $R_{NIR}=70$) to identify the correct dominant gas, with higher cloud fraction increasing this threshold. Reducing near-IR coverage to 1.4 $μ$m prevents recovery of the correct $N_2$-dominated solution across the explored SNR range, and instead favors a spurious $O_2$-dominated, $CO_2$-rich atmosphere. These results support near-IR coverage to $\sim1.7$ $μ$m and SNR $\gtrsim20$ at 0.75 $μ$m for robust oxygen interpretation on Earth-like planets.
