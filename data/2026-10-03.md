### [1] [Constraining Particle Stiffness in Asteroid Regolith from Early-Time High-Speed Penetrator Dynamics under Local Granular Variability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01470)
*Zichen Wang,Yang Yu,Chenyang Huang,Zhijun Song,Yutian Wu,Xiaojing Zhang,Xuhui Zhang*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 通过EDEM-Adams双向耦合离散元-多体模型，研究高速侵彻体早期动力学能否约束碎屑堆小行星风化层颗粒杨氏模量E，并筛查六个参数的影响。

Motivation: 碎屑堆小行星风化层的力学性质难以约束，因为接触响应既依赖材料属性又依赖局部颗粒构型；需探索高速侵彻早期动力学能否用于约束颗粒刚度（杨氏模量E）。

Method: 采用EDEM-Adams双向耦合离散元-多体模型，先筛查杨氏模量、泊松比、恢复系数、静摩擦系数、滚动摩擦系数和黏附强度六个参数；再在同一已沉降颗粒床的17个相同空间位置测试5个杨氏模量水平，共85次模拟并进行位置匹配比较。

Result: 在75–90 m/s速度范围内，改变杨氏模量对探针减速产生最清晰、最系统的差异，其他参数影响较弱；留一位置验证表明早期响应更适于区分较宽的模量区间而非相邻模量水平；介观分析将该差异归因于界面载荷分担集中及随E增大而扩展的三维高容量接触路径。

Conclusion: 结果为从高速侵彻响应缩小颗粒杨氏模量范围提供数值依据，尽管存在局部颗粒变异性；定量应用于真实小行星风化层仍需进一步实验验证。

Abstract: Mechanical properties of rubble-pile asteroid regolith remain poorly constrained because contact responses depend on both material properties and local particle configuration. This study examines whether early-time high-speed penetrator dynamics can constrain particle stiffness, represented by the particle Young's modulus E, within a controlled discrete-element model. A bidirectionally coupled EDEM-Adams discrete-element-multibody model is used to screen six parameters: Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, coefficient of restitution, static friction coefficient, rolling friction coefficient, and adhesion strength. Five Young's-modulus levels are then tested at the same 17 spatial locations in one settled granular bed, giving 85 simulations with location-matched comparisons across modulus levels. Within the 75-90 m s^-1 speed range, varying Young's modulus yields the clearest and most systematic differences in probe deceleration, whereas the other parameters have weaker effects over the tested ranges. Validation by withholding all modulus cases at one location in turn indicates that the early response is more informative for distinguishing broad modulus ranges than adjacent modulus levels. Mesoscale analysis links the response differences to concentrated interface load sharing and spatially extended three-dimensional high-capacity contact paths as Young's modulus increases. The results provide a numerical basis for narrowing the particle Young's-modulus range from high-speed penetration responses despite local granular variability. Quantitative application to real asteroid regolith requires further experimental validation.
