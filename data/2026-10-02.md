### [1] [How eccentric is the debris disk of epsilon Eridani? ALMA reveals a near-circular belt](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00462)
*Oto Ulrich,Joshua B. Lovell,David J. Wilner,Antranik A. Sefilian,Mark Booth*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 重新分析ε Eridani的ALMA Band 6数据，测量外碎屑带的偏心率，并约束额外行星的质量与轨道。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ε Eridani拥有最近的碎屑盘和类木星行星b，需检验外碎屑带是否存在可能由行星-盘相互作用引起的非零偏心率。

Method: 考虑受迫偏心率(e_f)与本征偏心率(e_p)两种模型，重新分析ALMA Band 6 (1.29 mm)观测，并结合Spitzer/IRAC、JWST/NIRCam F444W与JWST/MIRI F2550W数据探索行星系统结构。

Result: 给出99.7百分位上限e_f<2.6%、e_p<6.8%，并发现4σ的受迫偏心率迹象e_f=1.62(+0.39/-0.40)%，为目前毫米波段最低的受迫偏心率上限；额外行星（b之外）质量被约束在约2e-4至0.3 M_Jup，轨道分离约6-60 au。

Conclusion: 外碎屑带近乎圆形且动力学冷，联合多波段数据对ε Eridani系统中行星-盘相互作用及额外行星给出严格约束。

Abstract: The nearby (3.2 pc) K2V star $ε$ Eridani hosts the closest detected debris disk and an exo-Jupiter analog ($ε$ Eridani b). In this work, we reanalyse ALMA Band 6 (1.29 mm) observations of $ε$ Eridani to assess whether its outer debris belt exhibits a non-zero eccentricity, potentially induced by planet-disk interactions. Considering two models, one that accounts for the disk's forced eccentricity ($e_\mathrm{f}$) and another that accounts for a proper eccentricity ($e_\mathrm{p}$), we place stringent (99.7th-percentile) upper limits of $e_\mathrm{f} < 2.6\%$ and $e_\mathrm{p} < 6.8\%$, respectively, and find tentative evidence for a non-zero forced eccentricity of $e_\mathrm{f} = 1.62^{+0.39}_{-0.40}\%$ at the $4σ$ level. This corresponds to the lowest millimeter-derived upper limit on the forced eccentricity of any debris disk to date, which, together with the low upper limit on the proper eccentricity, suggests that the outer belt is nearly circular and dynamically cold. We further explore planetary system architectures that are consistent with these ALMA-based results in combination with Spitzer/IRAC, JWST/NIRCam F444W, and JWST/MIRI F2550W data. Together, these analyses provide insight into potential planet-disk interactions in this nearby system, constraining any additional planet beyond $ε$ Eridani b to masses ranging from $\sim 2\times10^{-4}$ to $0.3\,M_\mathrm{Jup}$ at orbital separations of $\sim 6$-$60$ au.

### [2] [Correlations between the Carbon-to-Oxygen Ratio and the Orbital and Physical Properties of Exoplanets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00813)
*Noah Ferich,Robert Royer,Cody Shakespeare,Jason H. Steffen*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文用REBOUND模拟不同C/O比盘中的行星形成，发现体密度可作区分硅富集与碳富集行星的指标，但单独使用不完全可靠。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 高C/O比原行星盘可能形成碳主导而非硅主导的类地行星；已有研究关注盘演化与行星形成，但未详细分析盘表面密度剖面差异如何影响系外行星系统的结构及铁/亲铁元素含量。

Method: 采用N体积分器REBOUND，并结合顺序尘埃凝结模型的数据，模拟不同C/O比系统中的行星形成。

Result: 整体体密度可用于区分硅富集行星与碳富集行星，但该指标单独使用时可能不够可靠。

Conclusion: 体密度是有用但非决定性的判别指标，需结合其他信息共同判断行星成分类型。

Abstract: Studies suggest that protoplanetary disks around stars with high carbon-to-oxygen (C/O) ratios can produce terrestrial planets with carbon-dominated compositions instead of silicon-dominated ones. While previous work studied the evolution of these disks and the planets that form within them, none analyze in detail how the differences in the surface density profiles of these disks could affect the architectures and iron and siderophile contents of exoplanetary systems. Here, we present a new study that uses the N-body integrator REBOUND and data from a sequential dust condensation model to simulate the formation of planets in systems with varying C/O ratios. We find that bulk density could be used to distinguish between silicon-rich and carbon-rich planets, though it may not be a reliable metric on its own.

### [3] [Planetary eccentricity enhances inward comet transport but suppresses gentle impacts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01053)
*Qunfeng Jiang*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 类地行星链中的适度偏心率会增加彗星向内输送，但会减少以低撞击速度（有利于前生命分子存活）到达的彗星比例，使前生命物质供给下降约30%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 彗星可为岩石行星输送挥发分和前生命物质，但脆弱分子的存活取决于大气进入与撞击过程。紧凑行星链能以低速将彗星向内转移，本文检验行星偏心率是否会破坏这一低速输送路径。

Method: 从八行星链中最外侧两颗行星之间区域出发，追踪64,000颗彗星至0.1太阳质量恒星宜居带内的一颗地球质量行星。另设模拟隔离向最内侧行星的最后转移，变化初始偏心率e=0–0.05及10、30倍互希尔半径间距，并用Δ=12对比10处近3:2周期比的影响。

Result: e=0.05时，最外侧行星拦截彗星比例下降11%，多6%彗星进入内区；最内侧行星撞击概率变化小于3%，但低于15 km/s的撞击比例从25%降至18%。所有注入彗星中低于15 km/s的撞击比例因此下降30%。撞击前速度测量显示偏心率优先去除最慢的接近。撞击化学模型给出HCN存活等效产率降低31%。

Conclusion: 适度行星偏心率可在增加向内输送的同时降低在化学有利撞击条件下输送的前生命物质，表明输送效率与前生命物质供给之间存在权衡。

Abstract: Comets can supply volatile and prebiotic material to rocky planets, but survival of fragile molecules depends on atmospheric entry and impact. Compact planet chains can transfer comets inward at low velocity. We test whether modest planetary eccentricity preserves this route. We follow 64,000 comets from the region between the two outermost planets of an eight-planet chain to an Earth-mass planet on a temperate orbit around a $0.1 M_\odot$ star. A second suite of simulations isolates the last transfer to the innermost planet across initial planet eccentricities $e_{p,0}=0$--$0.05$ and separations of 10 and 30 mutual Hill radii; a $Δ=12$ comparison tests the influence of the near-3:2 period ratio at $Δ=10$. At $e_{p,0}=0.05$, the fraction of comets intercepted by the outermost planet falls by 11 per cent and 6 per cent more reach the inner region. The innermost planet's impact probability changes by less than 3 per cent, while the fraction of impacts below 15 km s$^{-1}$ falls from 25 to 18 per cent. The fraction of all injected comets producing impacts below 15 km s$^{-1}$ consequently decreases by 30 per cent. Measurements of the comet-planet speed before the final close encounter show that eccentricity preferentially removes the slowest approaches. Applying an impact-chemistry model gives a 31 per cent lower HCN survival-equivalent yield. Modest planetary eccentricity can therefore increase inward transport while reducing the prebiotic material delivered under chemically favourable impact conditions.

### [4] [Interpreting effective homogeneous rheologies through a local differential map with application to the Moon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02026)
*Yeva Gevorgyan*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 提出局部分解方法，构建物理参数到等效均匀流变参数的传递矩阵，识别哪些内部层参数在Love数响应中可见。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分层黏弹性内部结构控制行星潮汐耗散的频率依赖，但长期轨道自旋积分中反复计算分层Love数代价高；等效均匀流变虽更便宜，但其有效参数本身无法指出所代表的内部层。

Method: 在固定几何基线上对分层到均匀的构造进行微分，仅扰动选定层的刚度和黏度；用二阶Love数的极点-留数表示构建物理到有效的传递矩阵；利用其奇异值、分辨率矩阵和模态主导矩阵判断可见扰动。

Result: 应用于五层月球模型：完整二阶响应对8个固体层流变参数中的7个敏感，近零方向为内核刚度，与流体外核屏蔽及Love数弱依赖核弹性结构一致；LLR频段内可见物理方向更少，一阶上弹性弹簧、孤立阻尼器和两个Voigt元件可重现九极点响应，有限扰动验证切向预测误差在百分之几内，有效半径受一对近简并长时标模态限制。

Conclusion: 该方法能将等效流变参数局域解释为具体内部层贡献，并识别响应中的可见与近零方向，为行星潮汐耗散的廉价建模提供诊断工具。

Abstract: Layered viscoelastic interiors control the frequency dependence of planetary tidal dissipation, but repeated layered Love-number calculations are expensive in long-term orbital and spin integrations. Equivalent homogeneous rheologies provide a cheaper representation of the same response, but their effective parameters do not by themselves identify the interior layers they represent. We develop a local interpretation of such parameters by differentiating the layered-to-homogeneous construction about a fixed-geometry baseline, perturbing only selected layer rigidities and viscosities. We use the pole-residue representation of the degree-two Love number to construct a physical-to-effective transfer matrix. We then use its singular values, resolution matrix, and mode-dominance matrix to determine which physical perturbations are visible in the Love-number response and how they appear in effective-rheology coordinates. Applied to a five-layer lunar model, the full degree-two response is sensitive to seven of eight solid-layer rheological parameters. The single near-null direction is the inner-core rigidity, consistent with shielding by the fluid outer core and with the weak dependence of Love numbers on core elastic structure. Over the LLR frequency interval, fewer physical directions are visible. To first order, the elastic spring, isolated dashpot, and two Voigt elements reproduce the nine-pole response over this interval. Finite perturbations confirm the tangent prediction to within a few percent, with the validity radius limited by a nearly degenerate pair of long-timescale modes.

</details>
