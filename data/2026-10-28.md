Motivation: 超短周期绕白矮星的褐矮星是热木星类比体,但其轨道倾角决定只能被遮挡有限纬度带,传统日食映射难以直接提取经度—纬度二维发射结构;需要一种能同时处理部分纬度被永久遮挡的信号分离方法,并评估 JWST 数据能否区分真实大气环流特征(如赤道急流)与模型系统性。

Brown dwarfs in ultra-short orbits around white dwarfs are an analog to hot Jupiters that can only ever be eclipsed within a limited range of latitudes. We adapt the eigenmapping method to disentangle the signals from the ever- and never-occulted regions. The result of this signal separation is a super-position of map structures, yielding sharp boundaries at the occultation region. We introduce a model that penalizes the harmonic complexity of the model map with Tikhonov regularization, whose parameters are tuned to reproduce the fit quality of the eigenmapping model. Such a model, however, is only statistically preferred at approximately $3 \times$ the signal-to-noise ratio of the JWST data. When fitting to the actual JWST data of WD 1202, the full-phase eigenbasis model is statistically preferred. However, the resulting model map of WD 1202 B does not resemble the GCM predictions, with a concentrated day-side hotspot at the sub-stellar point with an angular size $\approx 15^\circ$. Interpreting this result more carefully requires follow-up work to disentangle systematics from true emission signals. This model framework provides a means to detect emission features from these rare types of eclipses, and ultimately to test predictions of models of atmospheric circulation across hot Jupiters and their BD analogs.

### [3] [Retention of primordial atmospheres through giant impacts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36446)
*Darius Modirrousta-Galian,Jun Korenaga*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 巨撞击同时加热行星内部并增强引力束缚；本文自洽建模表明引力束缚能增幅大于幔部热能增幅，因此巨撞击倾向于促进大气保留而非逃逸，较大的、携带大气的行星更易通过碰撞增长形成，亚海王星的岩石内核平均比超级地球更重。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 地球大小及更大的岩石行星在形成的最后阶段预期会经历一次或多次巨撞击，部分发生在原行星盘消散之后，可能通过升高表面温度导致原始富氢大气流失。但巨撞击究竟促进大气流失还是保留尚不清楚，因为它既加热行星内部，又增强大气引力束缚；两者一直被割裂处理，缺乏自洽的对比评估。

Method: 建立岩石行星在其盘后最大一次巨撞击之后的演化模型，自洽地同时考虑引力束缚能的增加与幔部热能的变化。关键的物理分配是：整个吸积质量都贡献于引力束缚能的增加，而只有部分撞击能量转化为幔部温度升高；幔部熔融以潜热形式消耗能量，撞击体的金属核只向幔部传递很少能量，并在深部保持过热状态。

Result: 巨撞击带来的引力束缚能增量大于幔部热能增量，使行星向大气保留的方向偏移。较大的、携带大气的行星优先通过碰撞增长出现；相反，未经历此类撞击的较小行星在持续恒星辐照下倾向于失去原始大气。

Conclusion: 在族群层面，这些结果暗示亚海王星平均而言比超级地球拥有更重的岩石内部。巨撞击是塑造行星大气存续与半径—质量关系的重要机制，可解释观测到的行星族群差异。

Abstract: Rocky planets the size of Earth or larger are expected to undergo one or more giant impacts during the final stages of their formation. Some of these collisions occur after protoplanetary disk dissipation, potentially driving the loss of primordial hydrogen-rich atmospheres through elevated surface temperatures. However, whether giant impacts promote atmospheric loss or retention remains unclear because they both heat planetary interiors and increase atmospheric gravitational binding. Here we model the evolution of rocky planets after their largest post-disk giant impact, accounting self-consistently for the concurrent increase in gravitational binding energy and mantle thermal energy. Whereas the entire accreted mass contributes to the increase in gravitational binding energy, only part of the impact energy raises mantle temperature. Mantle melting consumes energy as latent heat, while the impactor's metallic core transfers little energy to the mantle and remains superheated at depth. Giant impacts therefore increase gravitational binding energy more than mantle thermal energy, shifting planets towards atmospheric retention. Larger, atmosphere-bearing planets emerge preferentially through collisional growth. By contrast, smaller planets without such impacts tend to lose their primordial atmospheres under sustained stellar irradiation. At the population level, these results suggest that sub-Neptunes have, on average, more massive rocky interiors than super-Earths.

Abstract: T. The likelihood-based analysis finds a positive amplitude difference up to $\sim 6100\,\mathrm{K}$ that becomes consistent with zero at higher temperatures, while the resampling analysis shows a similar but less pronounced trend. This result is qualitatively consistent with independent $v\sin i$ analyses suggesting low obliquities for cooler super-Earth and sub-Neptune hosts and broader obliquity distributions for hotter ones. This may also indicate a gradual change in the obliquity distribution with stellar temperature, although changes in spot distributions or selection effects could also contribute to the photometric trends in hot stars.
