Result: 1L2S因拟合较差被排除；2×1L1S因事件率低、总源流量与颜色不合理被否定。2L1S解释下两峰爱因斯坦时标为8.343±0.076天与3.231±0.047天，质量比q=0.15；主透镜可能为低质量M矮星，伴星为低质量褐矮星或超级木星，投影间距约450 au。

Conclusion: 该发现是目前微引力透镜探测到的最宽低质量伴星，凸显了识别极宽低质量伴星的潜力与挑战。未来Roman与Earth 2.0等设施将提高灵敏度，但克服简并、区分变星现象仍是建立稳健统计样本的关键。

Abstract: To search for very wide-orbit binary-lens events, we cross-match microlensing events detected by OGLE-IV and KMTNet between 2011 and 2024. After removing contaminants from variable stars, we identify OGLE-2016-BLG-0523 as a microlensing event showing two short-lived bumps in its light curve, with the two separated by 8.36 yr. We investigate three scenarios to explain the two bumps, including a binary-lens single-source (2L1S), a single-lens binary-source (1L2S), and two unrelated single-lens single-source events (2$\times$1L1S). The 1L2S interpretation is excluded due to its poorer fit to the data, while the 2$\times$1L1S scenario is disfavored by the low event rate, unphysical total source flux, and unphysical color for one of the two putative sources. For the surviving 2L1S interpretation, the Einstein timescales of the two bumps are $8.343 \pm 0.076$ days and $3.231 \pm 0.047$ days, respectively, implying a binary mass ratio of $q = 0.15$. A Bayesian analysis indicates that the primary lens is most likely a low-mass M dwarf, while the companion is probably a low-mass brown dwarf or super-Jupiter. The inferred projected separation is about 450 au, making the companion the widest low-mass object discovered via microlensing to date. Our discovery highlights both the promise and challenges of identifying very wide, low-mass companions through microlensing. Although upcoming facilities such as Roman and Earth 2.0 space telescopes will improve sensitivity to such events, overcoming degeneracies and distinguishing them from variable phenomena will remain essential for building a robust statistical sample.
