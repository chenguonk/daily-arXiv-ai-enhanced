### [2] [Orbital Stability of Apsidally Aligned Planetary Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21117)
*Gabriel Teixeira Guimarães,Eiichiro Kokubo*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 该论文研究致密行星系统动力稳定性中"拱线对齐"的物理作用,发现行星偏心率的系统性分量与随机分量起根本不同的动力学作用:随机分量驱动共振重叠导致混沌,而系统性分量在拱线对齐保护下几乎不影响稳定性,但该保护机制存在临界偏心率的有限有效域。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 致密行星系统的动力稳定性已知强烈依赖于轨道偏心率,但拱线对齐(apsidal alignment)的物理作用仍鲜为人知。作者希望厘清偏心率的不同组成部分如何分别影响稳定性,并解释为何部分高偏心率系统仍然稳定。

Method: 采用直接N体模拟,将各行星的偏心率分解为两部分:所有行星共享的系统性分量(systematic component),以及决定拱线对齐程度的随机分量(random component)。通过调节这两分量并监测共振重叠、混沌演化与拱线进动一致性,考察不稳定性时标的变化。

Result: 随机偏心率通过将相邻行星对推入平运动共振重叠区而驱动混沌演化,使系统失稳;系统性偏心率在很宽的取值范围内对不稳定性时标影响很小,因为行星轨道保持拱线对齐、相对轨道取向近乎固定。因此初始处于拱线对齐的系统稳定性接近初始圆轨道系统。该保护机制具有有限的有效域:当系统性偏心率超过某一临界值,相干拱线进动被破坏,出现差异进动,不稳定性时标迅速下降。

Conclusion: 致密行星系统的长期稳定性不能仅由轨道偏心率刻画,还取决于相干拱线进动(拱线对齐)的维持。该结论为建立新的解析稳定性判据提供了方向,提示应将拱线对齐状态纳入稳定性分析框架。

Abstract: The dynamical stability of compact planetary systems is known to depend strongly on orbital eccentricities, but the physical role of apsidal alignment remains poorly understood. We investigate this problem using direct {\em N}-body simulations by separating planetary eccentricities into a systematic component shared by all planets and a random component that determines the degree of apsidal alignment. We find that these two components play fundamentally different dynamical roles. Random eccentricity destabilizes planetary systems by driving neighboring planet pairs into the regime of mean-motion resonance overlap, leading to chaotic evolution. In contrast, systematic eccentricity has little effect on the instability timescale over a wide range of values because the planetary orbits remain in apsidal alignment, preserving nearly fixed relative orbital orientations despite finite eccentricities. As a result, systems that start in apsidal alignment are nearly as stable as initially circular systems. This protection mechanism has a finite domain of validity. Beyond a critical systematic eccentricity, coherent apsidal precession breaks down, differential precession develops, and the instability timescale decreases rapidly. Our results demonstrate that the long-term stability of compact planetary systems cannot be characterized solely by orbital eccentricities but also depends on the maintenance of coherent apsidal precession, providing a new direction for analytical stability criteria.
