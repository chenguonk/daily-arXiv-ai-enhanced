Abstract: We present a sensitivity analysis of three recent JWST direct imaging programs (GO-4050, GO-5835, and SURVEY-6005) designed to build a demographic study of sub-Jupiter mass ($0.1 - 1$ M$_{\mathrm{J}}$) exoplanets in nearby young associations and moving groups. We find that these programs are sensitive to sub-Saturn to Jupiter mass exoplanets with effective temperature (T$_\mathrm{eff}$) as low as $\lesssim 250$ K, already at $\sim 2$ arcsec. A comparison with the SHINE survey confirms that the improvements in mass sensitivity provided by JWST high-contrast imaging surveys outpace current ground observation for masses $\lesssim 1$ M$_{\mathrm{J}}$ and separations between $\sim20-500$ au, opening up a novel parameter space to explore. Overall, this analysis provides a robust framework for interpreting future exoplanet detections. By correlating photometrically derived properties (e.g. mass, $T_{\mathrm{eff}}$, and separation) with theoretical model predictions, we show how the access to potentially low detectable masses (sub-Saturn to Jupiter) strongly depends on the expected atmospheric chemistry of the exoplanet population.

### [16] [Compositions of Exoplanetary Debris Shaped by Milky Way Stellar Abundance Patterns](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21583)
*Isabella L. Trierweiler,Kendra Nguyen,Lou Baya Ould Rouis,Pratik Gandhi,Laura K. Rogers,Carey M. Lisse,Malena Rice,Damanveer S. Grewal,Edward Young*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 利用70颗污染白矮星样本，检验银河系化学演化(GCE)对系外行星物质成分的影响，发现铁质量分数随年龄和轨道动力学激发程度呈趋势，与主序星预测一致。

Motivation: GCE理论预测系外行星成分应随宿主星元素丰度在大尺度上变化，反映银河系恒星形成与元素增丰历史，但缺乏直接观测检验；污染白矮星可探测行星碎片的整体元素丰度，是理想探针。

Method: 采用文献中70颗污染白矮星样本，计算吸积物质的铁质量分数，并将其与白矮星前身星初始丰度的示踪量（年龄、运动学）比较；同时用主序星样本展示理论预测趋势，再与白矮星样本对照。

Result: 主序星预测趋势在污染白矮星样本中总体得到体现：氢主导白矮星在更年轻、动力学激发更弱的系统中铁质量分数偏大；氦主导白矮星中该趋势较弱，可能源于其吸积相位观测的不确定性。

Conclusion: 结合白矮星污染成分与恒星参数及运动学，可有效区分行星成分的大尺度（GCE）与局地驱动因素。

Abstract: Galactic chemical evolution (GCE) predicts large-scale variations in exoplanet compositions with host star elemental abundances, reflecting the Milky Way's history of star formation and elemental enrichment from stellar feedback. We investigate the impact of GCE on the compositions of exoplanetary bodies using polluted white dwarfs (WDs), which probe the bulk elemental abundances of planetary debris. Using a literature sample of 70 polluted WDs, we calculate iron mass fractions for the accreted bodies and compare these to tracers of the WD progenitor's initial abundances, including age and kinematics. We demonstrate predicted trends using a sample of main sequence stars, and we show that these are generally reflected in the polluted WD sample. In particular, H-dominated WDs show preferentially larger iron mass fractions amongst younger and less dynamically excited systems. This trend is weaker amongst He-dominated WDs, and we show that this may be attributed to uncertainties in their observed phases of accretion. Continuing to combine WD pollution compositions with stellar parameters and kinematics could be a valuable way to constrain large-scale versus local drivers of planetary compositions.
