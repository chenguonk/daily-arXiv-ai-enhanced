Method: 结合Keck/LRIS天体测量与VLT/FORS2四滤光片光度测量，进行Bus-DeMeo分类比对、来源模型概率分析，并从JPL解#2协方差中抽取10,000个克隆轨道，各向积分5 Myr。

Result: SDSS等效颜色为g-r=0.8254、r-i=0.1764、i-z=-0.4975 mag，最接近V型；在V型中位反照率0.362下，H=18.83 mag对应直径约0.379 km。来源模型赋予内主带输送通道合计95.4%概率。前100 kyr族群比例为Ayló'chaxnim 30.2%/Atira 69.8%，后100 kyr为3.6%/96.0%。前5 Myr积分中14.56%克隆与金星碰撞、3.77%与水星、0.22%与地球。

Conclusion: V型颜色与来源概率共同支持2025 SC79源自内主带，其轨道克隆在两族群间迁移，表明Atira与Ayló'chaxnim之间存在动态联系。

Abstract: Asteroids near the boundary between the Atira and 'Ayló'chaxnim classes can trace dynamical links between the two populations, but their small solar elongations make recovery and physical characterisation difficult. We recover 2025 SC$_{79}$, measure its optical colours, and investigate its likely source region and transitions between these populations. We combine Keck/LRIS astrometry and VLT/FORS2 four-filter photometry with Bus-DeMeo taxonomic comparisons, published source-model probabilities, and integrations of 10,000 clones drawn from the covariance of JPL solution #2 and followed for 5 Myr in each direction. The SDSS-equivalent colours are $g-r=0.8254_{-0.1339}^{+0.2136}$, $r-i=0.1764_{-0.0540}^{+0.0523}$ and $i-z=-0.4975_{-0.1162}^{+0.1055}$ mag. The V-type class mean gives the closest colour match. At the V-type median albedo $p_V=0.362$, $H=18.83\pm0.15$ mag implies a diameter of $0.379^{+0.027}_{-0.025}$ km. The source model assigns a combined probability of 95.4% to delivery channels associated with the inner Main Belt. The ensemble-mean 'Ayló'chaxnim/Atira residence fractions are 30.2%/69.8% over the preceding 100 kyr and 3.6%/96.0% over the following 100 kyr; the remaining 0.4% of future ensemble time follows collision removal. Clones spend an average of 231 kyr in the 'Ayló'chaxnim class over the preceding 500 kyr and 120 kyr over the following 500 kyr. In the 5-Myr forward integrations, 14.56% of clones collide with Venus, 3.77% with Mercury and 0.22% with Earth. The V-like colours and source-model probabilities favour an origin in the inner Main Belt for 2025 SC$_{79}$. Its clones move between the 'Ayló'chaxnim and Atira populations.
