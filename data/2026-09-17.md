### [1] [Evidence for an Extended Hydrogen Outflow on WASP-12 b](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17675)
*Morgan Saidel,Shreyas Vissapragada,Heather A. Knutson,Aaron Householder,Morgan MacLeod,Adam Jensen,Howard Isaacson,Judah Van Zandt,Fei Dai*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 用Keck Planet Finder观测WASP-12 b，发现凌星期间及前后存在Hα和Hβ吸收，支持其逃逸气体溢出洛希瓣并被潮汐剪切成延展尾迹的模型。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 早期型恒星周围行星的逃逸观测显示洛希填充因子越高、质量损失率越大，且部分行星存在领先/尾随尾迹。三维外流几何可约束外流物理、恒星风性质与轨道演化，而WASP-12 b是验证此类尾迹的第四个目标。

Method: 用Keck I上的Keck Planet Finder对WASP-12 b进行约8小时、以凌星为中心的连续观测，在恒星静止参考系中测量Hα和Hβ吸收信号及其时间分布。

Result: 探测到Hα和Hβ吸收，凌星时最强（Hα振幅2.66±0.25%，Hβ振幅2.88±0.43%），且存在凌星前、后吸收的证据。

Conclusion: 观测结果与外流模型一致：逃逸气体溢出洛希瓣、被平流带入恒星静止参考系后被潮汐剪切为延展尾迹；该行为与以往WASP-12 b的Hα观测及其他早期型恒星旁气态巨行星的延展外流定性相似。

Abstract: Recent observations of atmospheric escape from planets orbiting early-type stars indicate that planets with higher Roche filling factors have significantly higher mass loss rates. For three of these planets, full-orbit observations have revealed the presence of large leading and trailing tails of escaping planetary material. These 3D outflow geometries encode information about the underlying outflow physics, and can also be used to constrain their stellar wind properties and to predict their long-term orbital evolution. We present new evidence for extended H$α$ and H$β$ absorption from escaping hydrogen for a fourth planet, WASP-12 b. We observed WASP-12 b with the Keck Planet Finder on Keck I over a period of approximately eight hours centered on the transit. We find a H$α$ and H$β$ absorption signal in the stellar rest frame that is strongest during the transit (H$α$ amplitude: $2.66\pm0.25\%$, H$β$ amplitude: $2.88\pm0.43\%$), with evidence of both pre- and post-transit absorption. This measurement is consistent with outflow models where the escaping gas overflows the planet's Roche lobe and is advected into the stellar rest frame before being tidally sheared into extended tails. This outflow behavior is qualitatively similar to previous H$α$ observations of this planet as well as observations of extended outflows from other gas giants orbiting early-type stars.

### [3] [Bridging magnetothermal winds and photoevaporation to model discs dispersal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18334)
*Giovanni Picogna,Barbara Ercolano*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 提出一个闭式两相模型，统一描述光致蒸发与MHD盘风驱动的原行星盘消散，揭示磁通量损失率Rm决定两种演化路径。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 光致蒸发与MHD盘风通常被分开建模，但全局模拟表明内盘二者实为单一磁热风，需要一个统一的、尊重该物理本质的解析模型。

Method: 构建单磁力线风+光致蒸发汇项的两相闭式模型；风基由辐照温度和穿透柱密度固定；采用自限磁通闭合Bz∝Σ^q（q≤1/2），并用磁雷诺数Rm参数化独立磁通损失；将基座锚定于等离子体等分（β_base~1）从恒星辐照推导。

Result: 得到两种机制：高效磁通损失（Rm≲1）使磁化前沿后退逾一个量级并打开光致蒸发间隙；磁通保留（Rm≫1）驱动前沿外移、维持吸积、推迟消散约2.7 Myr。冷发射近似在盘演化早期成立；β_base~1将辐照对磁杠杆臂的影响限制在磁热环内，使峰值吸积率与入射通量脱耦。

Conclusion: 两种机制均以由内向外的方式清空星盘，分别通过光致蒸发放大的腔壁或扩张的磁热前沿实现；统一了先前分离的两种消散图像。

Abstract: Protoplanetary disc dispersal is driven by two processes usually modelled separately: photoevaporative and magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) disc winds. Global simulations indicate that in the inner disc these are not distinct outflows but a single magnetothermal wind. We assemble a closed-form, two-phase model that respects it. A single-field-line wind, whose base is fixed by the irradiated temperature and penetration column, supplies the launch and feeds a secular evolution, with photoevaporation convolved on as a sink. The flux closure $B_z\proptoΣ^q$ is self-limiting: for $q\le1/2$ depletion alone cannot demagnetise the disc, so dispersal requires independent flux loss, parameterised by the magnetic Reynolds number $\mathcal{R}_m$. Integrating the coupled system yields two regimes. Efficient flux loss ($\mathcal{R}_m\lesssim1$) lets the magnetisation front recede by over an order of magnitude and opens a photoevaporative gap. Flux retention ($\mathcal{R}_m\gg1$) drives the front outward, sustains accretion, and defers dispersal by $\approx2.7$~Myr. Deriving the base from stellar irradiation instead of prescribing it, we find that the cold-launch approximation is valid during the early stages of disc evolution: anchoring the base at plasma equipartition ($β_{\rm base} \sim 1$) confines irradiation's influence on the magnetic lever arm to the magnetothermal annulus, decoupling the peak accretion rate from the incident flux. Both regimes clear the disc inside-out, through either a photoevaporatively amplified cavity wall or an expanding magnetothermal front.

### [4] [Characterisation of chaos in meteoroid streams. Application to the Taurids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18420)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 金牛座流星雨流结构复杂，其演化主导机制尚未明确，需要识别塑造其动力学演化的关键机制。

Method: 采用混沌图与数值模拟，并结合半解析分析验证所识别机制；同时研究非引力力的影响。

Result: 北金牛座流星雨流受von Zeipel-Lidov-Kozai机制支配，并与多个木星平动共振相互作用：共振捕获粒子倾角增幅较小；初始半长轴越大效应越强；7:2共振可产生可观测的粒子群；非引力力模糊共振影响但不影响该机制；南金牛座与之高度相似。

Abstract: Aims. We explore the dynamics of the Northern and Southern Taurid meteoroid streams to identify major mechanisms structuring the evolution of the streams. Methods. We use chaos maps and numerical simulations to explore the dynamics of the streams. We also perform some semi-analytical analysis to confirm the mechanisms we identified in the maps. Results. We show how Northern Taurids are structured by the von Zeipel-Lidov-Kozai mechanism and how it interacts with several mean-motion resonances with Jupiter. This mechanism is visible as an increase in inclination for most particles, which is less prominent for those trapped in mean-motion resonances. It is especially strong for larger initial semi-major axis within the stream. We specifically study the 7:2 mean-motion resonance with Jupiter to confirm it leads to observable swarms. Furthermore, we explore the influence of non-gravitational forces, showing that they blur the influence of mean-motion resonances but not the von Zeipel-Lidov-Kozai mechanism. We also show that the dynamics of the Southern Taurids are very similar to the Northern Taurids.
