### [1] [On the Detectability of Compact Multi-Planet Systems via Gravitational Microlensing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05535)
*Stephen R. Kane*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文定量计算了TRAPPIST-1式紧凑多行星系统作为透镜时的微引力透镜信号，发现信号仅由八极矩主导（~1e-6），七个行星合计Δχ²~1e-3，远低于探测阈值，Roman望远镜无法通过微引力透镜探测此类系统。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 紧凑多行星系统（如TRAPPIST-1）可能是类地行星的主要族群，但其人口统计缺乏约束；单个近距离行星预期逃逸微引力透镜探测，而紧凑系统能否产生可探测的"集体"信号尚未被量化。

Result: 行星位于s=0.010–0.051 θ_E，处于透镜区内侧12–63倍；残差|δA/A|~1e-6，七星合计Δχ²~1e-3（即使1 mmag精度），低于探测阈值五个数量级；需将行星质量提高约300倍才可探测，而质量提高约7倍共振链即失去Hill稳定性保证；仅当透镜距源或太阳约15 pc内时外行星才进入透镜区。

Conclusion: Roman空间望远镜无法通过微引力透镜探测TRAPPIST-1式系统，但其同天区的凌星通道可大量探测此类行星，这标志着银河系普查中微引力透镜与凌星灵敏度之间的清晰分界。

Abstract: Compact multi-planet systems orbiting ultracool dwarfs, such as TRAPPIST-1, may be a major population of terrestrial worlds with poorly constrained demographics. Individual close-in planets are expected to escape microlensing detection, but whether a compact system can produce a detectable collective signal remains to be quantified. We calculate the microlensing signal from each TRAPPIST-1 planet, and of all seven combined, if the system were a lens toward the bulge. For a 0.09 $M_\odot$ lens at 4 kpc, $R_E \approx 1.21$ AU, placing the planets at $s = 0.010$--$0.051~θ_E$, 12--63 times interior to the lensing zone. We measure each signal as the residual after fitting a single-lens model, which accounts for the barycentric dipole. Because the quadrupole term cancels between the two images to first order, the residual scales as the octupole ($q s^3$), reaching $|δA / A| \sim 10^{-6}$. The seven planets combined yield $Δχ^2 \sim 10^{-3}$ even at 1~mmag precision, five orders of magnitude below the standard detection threshold. Detection requires increasing all planet masses by a factor of $\sim$300, whereas the resonant chain loses its guarantee of Hill stability at a factor of $\sim$7. Only a lens within $\sim$15 pc of the source or the Sun places the outer planets in the lensing zone. The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is therefore not expected to detect a TRAPPIST-1-like system through microlensing, whereas its transit channel in the same fields can detect such planets in large numbers, marking a sharp boundary in the Galactic census between microlensing and transit sensitivity.

Abstract: Ganymede is the only Solar System satellite with a global magnetic field driven by a core dynamo. Although its small moment-of-inertia (MoI) factor indicates a differentiated structure of a metallic core, rocky mantle, and outer hydrosphere, its exact bulk composition and layer thicknesses remain highly uncertain. We developed a comprehensive, long-term thermochemical evolution model that couples thermal history with magnetic field generation. Building upon previous theoretical frameworks, this study systematically evaluates how diverse chondritic precursor compositions constrain Ganymede's internal thermochemical evolution and core solidification processes. Consequently, this coupled thermochemical approach highlights the main novelty of this study: characterizing the hydrosphere's density serves as an independent constraint to break the structural degeneracy of Ganymede's bulk composition. Our model indicates that L and CI chondrites yield parameter spaces capable of sustaining comparable field strengths under a low-density hydrosphere scenario (1,200 kg m-3), whereas the H chondrite composition can satisfy this criterion under a high-density scenario (1,300 kg m-3). These predictions provide a critical framework to be tested by the JUICE mission arriving in 2031.
