TL;DR: 本文研究了不同恒星耀斑有效温度（9,000 K 与 19,000 K）对前寒武纪类地行星（0.01 bar O₂）大气光化学和生物标志物探测的影响，发现更热的耀斑对 O₃ 光化学影响更大，且耀斑温度对 M 星与 K 星大气的影响方向不同——19,000 K 耀斑可使 K2V 星行星的 0.2 μm 臭氧特征增强约 2 倍，却使 M2.5V 星行星该特征减弱 50%。

Motivation: 现有的恒星耀斑大气光化学模型通常假设约 9,000 K 的光谱，但近期多波段观测表明耀斑温度范围可达 4,000-40,000 K，而不同温度耀斑的发生率仍属未知。因此有必要探讨耀斑有效温度对类地行星大气化学和潜在生物标志物可探测性的影响。

Method: 对一颗含 0.01 bar O₂ 的前寒武纪类地行星，在反复耀斑条件下进行大气光化学演化建模。设计了四种情景：两种宿主恒星类型（K2V 和 M2.5V）× 两种耀斑温度（9,000 K 和 19,000 K），覆盖潜在参数空间。模拟 0.2-1.0 μm 的反射光光谱以评估生物标志物特征变化。

Result: 更热的耀斑（19,000 K）对两种恒星类型的 O₃ 光化学影响均更大。M 星行星大气更不稳定，O₃ 产生与破坏速率快速变化；K 星行星大气更稳定，仅受最热耀斑影响，更有利于生物标志物搜寻。19,000 K 耀斑对 O₃ 的影响方向取决于恒星光谱类型：K2V 星使 0.2 μm 特征增强约 2 倍，M2.5V 星则使其降低 50%。

Conclusion: 耀斑有效温度是光化学模型中不可忽略的关键参数，不同恒星类型下耀斑温度对 O₃ 的影响方向可能相反。未来任务（如 EVE SMEX）应提供更精确的耀斑温度约束，以改进光化学模型，为 HWO 生物标志物观测提供更可靠的预测。

Abstract: Atmospheric photochemical models incorporating the impacts of stellar flares often assume a $\sim$9,000 K spectrum at ultraviolet-optical wavelengths. Recent multiwavelength observations, however, reveal a more complex picture with temperature measurements spanning 4,000-40,000 K, although the occurrence rates for flares with different temperatures remain unknown. Here, we model the evolution of a Proterozoic Earth-like world with 0.01 bar of O$_2$ under repeated flaring to identify the impact of flare effective temperatures. We explore four scenarios - two host star types (K2V and M2.5V) and two flare temperatures (9,000 K and 19,000 K) - selected to bound the potential parameter space. The hotter flares have a larger impact on O$_3$ photochemistry for both stellar types. M-star planetary atmospheres are more volatile and exhibit rapid changes in their O$_3$ production and destruction rates. Meanwhile, K-star planetary atmospheres are more stable and are only impacted by the hottest flares, proving advantageous for biosignature searches. We simulate 0.2-1.0 $μ$m reflected light spectra for all four scenarios, and find that 19,000 K flares can result in either production or destruction of O$_3$ depending on the host star spectral type increasing the 0.2 $μ$m feature by $\sim$2$\times$ for the K2V star but decreasing it by 50% for the M2.5V star. Future missions such as the EVE SMEX mission concept will provide robust flare temperature constraints for young FGKM stars, which will serve as inputs to improve photochemical models to inform future HWO observations.
