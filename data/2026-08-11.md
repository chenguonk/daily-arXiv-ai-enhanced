### [1] [What can Planetary Ice Shells Teach Us about Ocean Mixing and Life, and Why Salt Matters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07826)
*Nicole Shibley*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文综合讨论了冰壳覆盖海洋系统的核心问题：由于无法直接测量冰下海洋，我们如何通过表面观测推断地下海洋的分层与混合过程，并进一步探讨盐度在这种相互作用中的关键作用，以及这些过程对宜居性和生命起源的启示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 冰壳覆盖的海洋系统（从地球北极海冰到太阳系冰卫星的全球冰壳）中，地下海洋过程的原位测量往往缺乏，因此需要依赖表面观测来推断冰下海洋的混合与分层特征。作者旨在整合地表观测、盐度控制作用与宜居性之间的关联。

Method: 本文采取综述与综合分析方法，将三类观念进行整合：(1) 利用过去和现在行星冰壳的表面观测反推地下海洋的分层与混合（反之亦然）；(2) 盐度是控制分层、混合及冰存在与否的关键因素；(3) 这种相互作用在跨行星系统背景下可能为宜居性和生命起源提供新见解。

Result: 提出一个统一框架，连接冰壳表面观测、海洋盐度控制的分层与混合过程，以及这些过程对行星宜居性和生命起源的潜在启示。

Conclusion: 海洋盐度是联系冰壳观测、海洋混合过程以及宜居性评估的关键纽带，综合表面观测与盐度效应可为理解冰下海洋环境及其生命潜力提供新的分析路径。

Abstract: Past and present climates, both on Earth and in planetary systems, maintain ice covers with underlying oceans. This can range from sea ice in the Arctic Ocean to global ice shells on ice-covered moons in the Solar System to ice sheets in past episodes of Earth's history. When an ice shell overlies an ocean, it becomes challenging to infer what ocean processes could be going on below as in-situ ocean measurements are often lacking. This means that what we learn about ocean mixing must often be inferred from above-surface measurements. Here we synthesize the following ideas: (1) surface observations of past and present planetary ice shells give insight into subsurface ocean stratifications and mixing (and vice versa), (2) the stratification, mixing, and presence of ice in (1) is largely controlled by the ocean salinity, and (3) the interplay between (1) and (2) in past and present planetary systems may provide new insights into habitability and the origins of life.

### [2] [Atmospheric Diversity of Giant Planet Analogs from 3-5um with SPHEREx](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08908)
*Rocio Kiman,Brendan P. Bowler,Jerry W. Xuan,Narisara Mayer,Claire Finley*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文利用SPHEREx全天巡天数据，构建了94个年轻（<300Myr）低重力L和T矮星的光谱测量目录，涵盖3-5um波段，研究其大气多样性，并与JWST合成光度进行比较。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 巨行星的发射光谱在3-5um热红外波段达到峰值，该波段包含丰富的碳化学、垂直混合、云层和成分信息，但从地面难以观测，且JWST每次目标需要独立指向，效率有限。SPHEREx提供了全天0.75-5um光谱数据，为大规模研究提供了机会。

Method: 利用SPHEREx数据构建94个年轻低重力L和T矮星的光谱测量目录，计算22个滤波器的合成JWST/NIRCam和MKO光度与颜色，分析物理相关的颜色、绝对星等和光谱型趋势。

Result: 发现最低重力天体通常具有最红的3-5um颜色，且在同一光谱型下比场L矮星更亮。部分巨行星紧随低重力天体轨迹，而其他巨行星显示出更大离散度和更极端的颜色，表明云性质、温度、金属丰度或垂直混合存在差异。

Conclusion: SPHEREx数据能够有效支撑亚恒星天体的群体级大气多样性研究，补充了JWST等设施在3-5um波段的观测能力，数据提取代码、光谱和合成光度均已公开。

Abstract: The emergent spectra of giant planets peak at thermal infrared wavelengths (3-5um) and possess atmospheric features in this regime that trace carbon chemistry, vertical mixing, clouds, and composition. However, this spectral region is challenging to access from the ground, and with JWST generally requires an independent pointing for each target. SPHEREx is providing all-sky spectra of millions of sources from 0.75-5um, enabling both individual and population-level studies of isolated and widely bound substellar objects. In this work we present a SPHEREx spectrophotometric atlas of 94 young (<300Myr) low-gravity L and T dwarfs to study their atmospheric diversity in the relatively unexplored wavelength range from 3-5um. These include free-floating planetary-mass objects in a range of ages, masses, and temperatures regularly probed with JWST and which will be accessible with upcoming instruments such as Keck/SCALES and ELT/METIS. We compute synthetic JWST/NIRCam and MKO photometry and colors across 22 filters, and examine trends in physically-motivated sets of colors, absolute magnitudes, and spectral types. We find that the lowest-gravity objects generally have the reddest 3-5um colors and tend to be brighter than field L dwarfs for a given spectral type, extending previous patterns originally established at near-infrared wavelengths. Compared to the low-gravity sample of free-floating objects, some giant planets closely follow the low-gravity locus, while others show larger scatter and more extreme colors, pointing to differences in cloud properties, temperature, metallicity, or vertical mixing. The SPHEREx data extraction code developed in this work is made available together with the extracted spectra and synthetic photometry.

### [3] [The Role of Extended-Source Geometry and Diurnal Cycles on Exoplanetary Thermal Baselines: Reconciling the Brightness Temperature of 55 Cancri e](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09241)
*Mradumay Sadh*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本研究将基于几何的InstellCa代码升级为InstellCa-2.0，用于计算行星给定纬度上的经度平均辐照度及其热廓线，并以55 Cancri e为案例验证了异步旋转对裸岩行星亮度温度估计的重要影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超短周期（USP）岩质行星可能表现出异步旋转，而辐照几何形状会影响其亮度温度的估计。55 Cancri e此前被假设存在异步旋转，且关于其是否存在大气层存在学术争议，因此需要精确的辐照模型来解释观测到的热特征。

Method: 将InstellCa代码扩展为InstellCa-2.0，将行星视为三维旋转体，考虑恒星天顶角在昼夜循环中的变化，计算给定纬度上的经度平均辐照度，并推导热廓线。以55 Cancri e作为专门案例研究，在裸岩行星和邦德反照率（A_B=0.3）假设下计算半球平均亮度温度。

Result: 几何模型计算的半球平均亮度温度与JWST MIRI对55 Cancri e的高精度亮度温度估计（1796 K）高度吻合。

Conclusion: 该结果自然解释了55 Cancri e观测到的热印记，并优雅地调和了此前关于该行星的研究工作，表明裸岩行星假设下异步旋转的几何模型可以解释观测数据。

Abstract: This study augments the geometry-based InstellCa code that calculates accurate irradiance values to InstellCa-2.0, which computes the longitude averaged instellation over a given latitude on the planet along with its thermal profile. The estimation of instellation is performed considering the planet as a 3D body that rotates with respect to the star with a varying zenith angle of the star across the diurnal cycle. We focus on the implications of irradiation geometry on a specific class of ultra-short period (USP) rocky planets that may exhibit asynchronous rotation. This serves as a specialised case study to demonstrate how rotation and proximity to host-stars can affect the estimated brightness temperatures of bare rocky worlds. 55 Cancri e is selected specifically to demonstrate the importance of this effect as it has been earlier hypothesised to exhibit asynchronous rotation and also has a debated scientific discourse about the existence of an atmosphere on the planet. The results show excellent agreement of the hemisphere-averaged brightness temperature calculated through the geometric model, under the bare rocky planet and the corresponding Bond albedo (A_B = 0.3) assumption, with the highly precise JWST MIRI brightness temperature estimate for 55 Cancri e (1796 K). This naturally offers an explanation of the observed thermal imprint that elegantly reconciles the previous works on the planet.

</details>


### [4] [A Machine Learning Based Search for Lunar Anomalies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09350)
*Cameron Kelahan,Daniel Angerhausen,Adam Lesnikowski,Valentin T. Bickel*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文利用Beta-Variational Autoencoder（VAE）无监督学习模型分析LRO自2009年以来采集的高分辨率月球影像，成功识别月球表面异常特征，包括地质构造（岩崩沉积、新鲜撞击坑、不规则月海斑块、火山坑/塌陷熔岩管）以及人造物体（着陆航天器），并显著定位了Plaskett陨石坑和Paracelsus C陨石坑等感兴趣区域及大量登陆技术资产。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LRO自2009年起积累了海量高分辨率月球影像数据集，为以空前规模研究月球表面提供了机会，但传统人工分析方法难以高效处理如此大规模的数据，因此需要测试无监督学习模型自动识别异常特征的能力。

Method: 采用Lesnikowski等人（2024）创建的Beta-Variational Autoencoder（VAE）无监督学习模型，对LRO窄角相机（约0.5-2米/像素）采集的月球表面高分辨率影像进行异常特征检测与定位。

Result: 模型成功识别出多种科学上有价值的异常特征（包括地质构造和人工物体），并以具有统计显著性的速率成功复原了两处感兴趣区域（Plaskett陨石坑和Paracelsus C陨石坑）以及众多着陆技术资产。

Conclusion: Beta-VAE无监督学习模型在月球表面异常特征检测方面表现出有效能力，既能发现科学意义的地质构造，也能定位人造物体，为大规模月球表面数据分析提供了有力工具。

Abstract: The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) has been collecting high-resolution images (at around 0.5-2 meters per pixel linearly with its Narrow Angle Camera) of the Moon since 2009, amassing a large dataset of images and offering researchers the opportunity to study the surface of the Moon at unprecedented scale. Here, we aim to test the abilities of the Beta-Variational Autoencoder (VAE) created by Lesnikowski et al. (2024), an unsupervised learning model which identifies anomalous features across the Moon's surface, locating not only scientifically useful geologic formations such as rockfall deposits, fresh impact craters, irregular mare patches, or volcanic pits/collapsed lava tubes, but also artificial objects such as landed spacecraft. This investigation further gauged the model's ability to locate anomalous surface features, successfully recovering two places of interest (Plaskett Crater and Paracelsus C Crater) and numerous landed technological assets at a statistically significant rate.

### [5] [TOI-7475 spectroscopic follow up - detection of a massive hot Jupiter on an eccentric orbit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09679)
*Joanna Pietrucha,Jakub Jerzy Tokarek,Mateusz Witczak,Wojciech Dimitrov*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文报告了对恒星TOI-7475的首次光谱后续观测，并探测到一颗行星质量伴星。通过TESS观测发现周期为3.25天的凌星信号，结合高分辨率光谱和Wilson-Devinney方法分析，确定伴星质量为12.0±1.0倍木星质量，将其归类为热木星，轨道偏心率为0.16。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对TESS探测到的候选凌星信号TOI-7475进行光谱后续观测验证，以确认其行星性质并表征其物理参数。

Method: 使用TESS光度数据发现周期3.25天的凌星信号（深度0.006星等，持续约4.5小时），编号为TOI-7475；利用PST1望远镜收集24条高分辨率阶梯光谱获取径向速度；采用Wilson-Devinney方法联合分析光度数据和径向速度。

Result: 确认伴星质量为12.0±1.0倍木星质量；由于其半长轴小、轨道周期短，被归类为热木星；轨道偏心率为0.16，光谱数据中显示出的椭圆轨道特征可能表明该星系较年轻。

Conclusion: TOI-7475是一颗围绕F5型恒星（约1.48倍太阳质量）运行的年轻热木星，其显著偏心率可能与其年轻年龄相关。

Abstract: We present the first spectroscopic follow-up for TOI-7475, and report the detection of a planetary-mass companion. Transits with a period of 3.25 days were observed by TESS. The dips in brightness are 0.006 mag and last approximately 4.5 hours. The host star is a spectral type F5 with a mass of approximately 1.48 M$_{\odot}$. We collected 24 high resolution echelle spectra using the PST1 telescope. Analysis of the TESS photometric data and our radial velocities was performed using the Wilson-Devinney method. We obtained a planetary mass of the companion of 12.0$\pm$1.0 M$_{\mathrm{jup}}$. The small semi-major axis and short orbital period classify the planet as a Hot Jupiter. Its orbit is elliptical with an eccentricity of 0.16. The ellipticity is evident in the spectroscopic data and could indicates the young age of the system.

</details>
