*Yanqin Wu,Xuqi Chen,Joshua Bromley*

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 基于流动不稳定性可提供的自由能量进行理论估计，导出粘性参数 alpha 与斯托克斯数 St、无量纲压力梯度 Π 及局部尘埃-气体密度比 ε 的显式关系，并辅以专门设计的数值模拟进行验证。

Result: 得到 alpha ~ St·Π²·f(ε)（St<1，f(ε) 为 ε 的显式函数），该估计能解释以往多项研究结果，并得到新增定制模拟的支持；文中还讨论了尘埃标高自洽解与压力丘的天体物理意义两个应用。

Abstract: In proto-planetary disks, gas turbulence can cluster dust grains and catalyze the formation of planetesimals. This process depends on the strength of the turbulence. Here, we evaluate the turbulence strength that is induced by the streaming instability. Based on the amount of free energy available, we estimate the viscous parameter to be alpha ~ St Pi^2 f(epsilon), where St (< 1) is the Stokes number of the streaming grains, Pi is the dimensionless pressure gradient, and f (epsilon) is an explicit function of epsilon, the local dust-to-gas density ratio. This estimate explains the results of many previous studies, and is also supported by our additional tailor-made simulations. We discuss two easy applications of our result, one is a self-consistent dust scale height, one is the astrophysical relevance of a pressure bump. Lastly, while this work allows one to characterize the streaming turbulence, we emphasize our current inability to connect the strength of turbulence to the formation of planetesimals.
